How it’s calculated

Only work on pallet layouts is counted, not the whole machine project. One rule:

number per year × (time today − time with Pallet Designer) × hourly cost

  • Recipe handling program: machines × (hours today − 8 hours with Pallet Designer)
  • New layouts, office: machines × layouts per machine × (design + PLC/HMI + robot − 3 minutes). The amounts on the three cards are the stages’ shares of this one saving
  • New layouts, site: today every layout is tried; with Pallet Designer one hour per machine
  • Layout changes: changes × the office time saved on a new layout, plus trips for changes × (time + cost of one trip)
  • Layout data errors: errors × time to fix, plus trips for errors × (time + cost of one trip)
  • Layouts for quotes: quotes × (minutes today − 10 minutes)
  • Configurator in the quote: revenue, not a saving; added only in the total
  • Days and months: hours ÷ 8 and hours ÷ 160

How it’s calculated

Only work on pallet layouts is counted. Two figures that add up: fees avoided and the value of staff time

  • Integrator job fees: layouts ordered from the integrator × fee per job
  • Service visits: visits × fee per visit
  • Briefing the integrator: jobs × (minutes today − 3 minutes) × internal hourly cost of plant staff
  • In-house layouts: layouts × (minutes today − 3 minutes) × internal hourly cost of plant staff
  • Days and months: hours ÷ 8 and hours ÷ 160
