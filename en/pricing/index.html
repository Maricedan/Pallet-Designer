FAQ

Questions about licences and pricing

What the licence includes, how a subscription differs from a perpetual licence, and what drives the price of the other system components

Are the prices net?

Yes. All amounts are net prices, VAT is added on top. The integrator licence costs from €2,800 per year for one design seat. The other system components are quoted in a written offer once the scope is agreed

What does the annual integrator licence include?

Full program functionality on one seat: designing layers and pallets, mixing products, testing gripper variants, layout validation, data export to the machine, files for robot simulation and PDF documentation

The price includes a user manual and team training. The program works without a permanent internet connection, and the licence can be transferred to another device

How does a perpetual licence differ from a subscription?

A subscription is an annual fee — after a year you renew the licence for the next period. A perpetual licence is a one-off payment after which the program runs with no further annual fees. Its value corresponds roughly to five years of subscription

The perpetual option pays off for teams that know they will use Pallet Designer across many projects. We quote it on request

Can I start with one licence and add seats later?

Yes. The simplest route is a single design seat. When more people need the program — quoting, design and commissioning in parallel — we prepare a team pack for several seats in one company. The pack is quoted on request, at better terms than the sum of individual licences

What determines the price of the end-customer configurator?

The scope of deployment: the number of machines, lines and plants where the end customer will create layouts independently, and the chosen option — a perpetual licence or an annual subscription

For the integrator it's a line item added to the machine quote with your own margin. The end customer gains independence, you gain a stronger offer

What determines the price of machine integration?

The controller platform and the scope of work. For the Siemens standard we have ready PLC function blocks, HMI screens and scripts, so integration is fastest there. Other control systems, additional export formats, MES/ERP integration or on-site training are quoted separately once the scope is agreed

Can I see the program before buying?

Yes. We arrange a 20-minute online demo in which we build a pallet layout in Pallet Designer using your data: format, pallet, products. After the demo you receive an offer tailored to the scope of your project
